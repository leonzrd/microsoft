Privacy Statement

This website is used for authorised security awareness and phishing simulation exercises.

During a simulation, the following information may be recorded for training and reporting purposes:

  • Recipient email address used for the simulation
  • Email opened, link clicked, and simulation form submitted events
  • Event timestamps
  • Basic browser, device, and operating system information

The simulation is not intended to collect, store, or retain real Microsoft account passwords or other account credentials.

Information collected through the simulation is used only for authorised security awareness training, reporting, and improvement of staff security practices.

If you have questions about this simulation or the information recorded, please contact Central Telecoms.
