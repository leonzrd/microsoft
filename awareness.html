This was a security awareness simulation. You clicked a simulated phishing link designed to show how convincing a real phishing email and sign-in page can look.

What should you check next time?

A few quick checks can help you spot a suspicious email before you click or enter any information.

01

Check the sender

Look closely at the full email address and domain, not just the display name.

02

Check the link

Before clicking, inspect where the link actually goes. Be cautious of unfamiliar or slightly altered domains.

03

Pause before signing in

Unexpected login prompts, urgency and requests for account details are good reasons to stop and verify first.

✓
No password was collected during this test. The simulation records the training interaction only. Any password entered into the test page is not retained.

Not sure? Ask first.

If an email, link or sign-in request doesn't look right, stop and verify it through a trusted contact or your IT support team.

Spotting and reporting suspicious emails is a good thing.
Taking a few seconds to check can prevent a real compromise.
